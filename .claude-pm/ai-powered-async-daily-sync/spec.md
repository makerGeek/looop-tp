# AI-powered async daily sync

## Brief
add ai-powered async daily sync

## Context (orchestrator-provided)

This work lands inside the `pm-app/` project on the current branch
`claude/pm-app-stage-1-tasks-inbox`. Stage 1 (tasks + projects + board +
inbox + Cmd-K + attachments) is in place. The vision doc
(`/root/.claude/plans/create-a-codecademy-inspired-dynamic-widget.md`,
Stage 2) frames this feature:

> Every workday a 30-second prompt arrives: yesterday / today / blockers.
> AI summarises the team's answers into a single morning digest, posted
> in the workspace. No standup meeting survives.

The data model sketch already names `async_standup (id, user_id, date,
yesterday, today, blockers)`. The Anthropic API powers the summary via
a Supabase Edge Function with prompt caching.

## Requirements (BA)

### 1. One-line summary
Written daily standup with an AI-generated team digest.

### 2. Why now
Stage 1 shipped the workspace primitives (tasks, board, inbox); the team
still relies on a synchronous standup meeting. A 30-second written ritual
plus an AI-summarised morning digest is the cheapest way to kill that
meeting and validate the "async by design" thesis.

### 3. User stories

1. **As a workspace member**, I want to submit a short daily entry
   (yesterday / today / blockers) for today's date, so that I share status
   without attending a meeting.
2. **As a workspace member**, I want to edit or overwrite my entry for
   today before the digest is generated, so that I can correct mistakes.
3. **As a workspace member**, I want to see today's morning digest summarising
   the team's entries in the workspace, so that I know what everyone is
   working on and where blockers are without reading every entry.
4. **As a workspace member**, I want to browse my own past entries by date,
   so that I can recall what I committed to and what I shipped.
5. **As a workspace member**, I want to see who has and hasn't submitted an
   entry today, so that the digest's coverage is transparent.
6. **As a workspace member**, I want the digest to regenerate (on demand or
   when more entries land) so that late submitters still appear in the
   summary.

### 4. Acceptance criteria

**Story 1 — submit entry**
- AC 1.1: Given I am signed in and a member of a workspace, when I open the
  daily sync surface for the first time today, then I see three empty
  prompts labelled *Yesterday*, *Today*, *Blockers* and a submit action.
- AC 1.2: Given I fill in at least one of the three fields, when I submit,
  then a single `async_standup` row is persisted for `(user_id, workspace_id,
  today's local date)` and the surface re-renders in "submitted" state.
- AC 1.3: Given any of the three fields is non-empty plaintext or markdown,
  when I submit, then the content is stored verbatim (no silent rewriting).
- AC 1.4: Given I have not submitted today, when I view the surface, then
  it is the entry form (not the digest).

**Story 2 — edit own entry**
- AC 2.1: Given I have submitted today's entry, when I open the surface
  again on the same local date, then my existing answers are pre-filled and
  editable.
- AC 2.2: Given I edit and re-submit, when the save completes, then the
  same row is updated in place (one entry per user per date, enforced).
- AC 2.3: Given the day has rolled over (user's local date changed), when I
  open the surface, then yesterday's entry is read-only and a new empty
  form appears for today.

**Story 3 — team digest**
- AC 3.1: Given at least one teammate has submitted today, when I view the
  workspace's daily sync surface, then a digest section is visible that
  summarises the team's entries into prose covering what shipped yesterday,
  focus for today, and outstanding blockers.
- AC 3.2: Given no teammate has submitted today, when I view the surface,
  then the digest section shows an empty state (e.g. "No entries yet today")
  and no AI call is made.
- AC 3.3: Given the digest exists, when I read it, then every claim is
  traceable back to a submitting teammate's entry (the digest names people
  for each item it attributes).
- AC 3.4: Given the digest is generated, when I view it, then it is scoped
  to my current workspace only (entries from other workspaces never appear).
- AC 3.5: Given the digest content is generated by AI, when I view it, then
  it is visibly labelled as AI-generated.

**Story 4 — history**
- AC 4.1: Given I have submitted entries on prior dates, when I navigate to
  my history view, then I see a chronological list of my past entries with
  the date and the three fields.
- AC 4.2: Given I view a past entry, when the date is older than today,
  then the entry is read-only.

**Story 5 — coverage transparency**
- AC 5.1: Given I view today's daily sync surface, when the team has N
  members, then I can see which members have submitted today and which have
  not (e.g. submitted / not submitted lists or counts).
- AC 5.2: Given a member is marked as "not submitted today", when they
  submit, then the coverage indicator updates without a full page reload
  on next view.

**Story 6 — digest regeneration**
- AC 6.1: Given the digest exists and a new entry is submitted after it,
  when a member triggers regeneration (explicit action or natural revisit
  after threshold), then a new digest is produced that includes the new
  entry.
- AC 6.2: Given regeneration is in progress, when I view the surface, then
  I see a loading state and the previous digest remains until the new one
  replaces it.
- AC 6.3: Given the digest is generated more than once on the same date,
  when I read it, then only the latest digest is shown (no duplicate
  digests displayed).

### 5. Non-goals

- **No Slack / email delivery.** The digest lives inside the workspace UI
  only. External notification channels (Slack post, email blast, push) are
  out of scope.
- **No reminders or scheduled prompts.** The app does not nag, email, or
  push-notify users to submit. Users discover the form by visiting the
  workspace.
- **No per-project standups.** One standup is per workspace, not per
  project, in this slice.
- **No editing other people's entries.** Members can only read teammates'
  entries via the digest (and history of their own).
- **No retros, weekly summaries, or trend dashboards.** Those belong to
  later vision stages.
- **No voice / video / file attachments on entries.** Plaintext or markdown
  text only.
- **No threaded replies or reactions on the digest.** It is a read artifact,
  not a conversation surface.
- **No timezone-aware "day" negotiation across teammates.** Each user's
  entry is keyed to their own local date; the digest is "today, in the
  reader's timezone" and accepts the inherent fuzziness.
- **No custom prompts or configurable templates.** The three fields are
  fixed at *yesterday / today / blockers*.

### 6. Open questions

1. Which Anthropic model should power the daily summary — Haiku for cost,
   or Sonnet for quality? Vision doc suggests Haiku; confirming.
2. Is the digest visible to all workspace members (including `guest` role
   memberships), or members-only? Defaulting to: same as other workspace
   reads (any `workspace_member` row).
3. Is there a hard cutoff time after which the day's digest "locks" (no
   more regeneration), or does it stay regenerable indefinitely?

(None marked BLOCKER — sensible defaults exist for all three and the
architect can decide if the user does not weigh in.)

### 7. Success signal

After one full work week of dogfooding inside one workspace, the team has
cancelled its recurring synchronous standup meeting and ≥ 70% of active
members submit an entry on a given workday without being asked.
